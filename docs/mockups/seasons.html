Walden Seasons

Three ways the seasonal backdrop could look. The same scene and the same text appear in each, so you can compare the look and how Thoreau's inner voice is presented. Pick a season to change all three. Buttons and the text field work as a preview.

Season
Direction A

Cut-paper landscape

Flat, layered shapes. Each season swaps the palette, the foliage and the snow, and the same drawing serves all ten seasons of the game. The cheapest to build and the easiest to extend with more props (a finished chimney, a bigger woodpile).

Thoreau

Direction B

Pencil journal

Graphite drawings with a thin watercolor wash for the season, a nod to the Thoreau family pencil business. The narrator is printed on the page. Thoreau's voice is handwritten in the margin, so you can tell the two apart at a glance. A small ledger doubles as the shop and house-cost widget.

Account of the season

Direction C

Living atmosphere

A painted skyline with a lit cabin window and animated weather: petals in spring, fireflies in summer, falling leaves in autumn, snow in winter. Text sits right on the scene. The most mood for the least art, though it is the least specific to the story.

Thoreau

Everything here is drawn in code as a placeholder. The final art could be hand-drawn, generated, or built up from the same layered pieces. The scene text is sample copy written for this mockup.
